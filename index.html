Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about investing in Mutual Funds through an MFD.

Why should I invest through an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor?
An AMFI Registered MFD like Parthasarathi Panda (ARN-185973) provides expert scheme selection, portfolio rebalancing guidance, emotional discipline during market crashes, and paperless assistance without charging you any direct advisory fee.
How safe are Mutual Fund investments? Where does my money go?
All mutual funds in India are strictly regulated by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India). Your investment money is transferred directly from your bank account to the Asset Management Company (AMC) custodian (like SBI Mutual Fund, HDFC Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential), ensuring 100% safety of your funds.
What is the minimum amount required to start a monthly SIP?
You can start a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) with as low as ₹500 or ₹1,000 per month. You can increase, decrease, or pause your SIP amount anytime without any penalty.
How do I redeem or withdraw money from my Mutual Fund?
Redemption is 100% online. With one click on your mobile phone or portal, your redeemed funds are directly credited into your registered bank account within 1 to 3 working days.
